Why this can mislead you

  • Trained mostly on upright PA films from outpatient and ward datasets. Supine, rotated, portable ICU films are out of distribution.
  • Lines and tubes can drive predictions. The model may learn "sick-patient hardware" as a shortcut for disease.
  • No tube-position label. It cannot tell you whether the ET tube, NG tube or CVC tip is in the right place.
  • Scores are not diagnoses. A heatmap shows where the model looked, not where the disease is.
