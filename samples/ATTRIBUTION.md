# Bundled teaching images — sources and licences

All images are from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded 24 Oct 2026. Each was converted to greyscale, resized to at most 1400 px on the long side and re-encoded as JPEG (no metadata carried over). No other changes were made. **Each image keeps its own licence** (listed below), which is not the CC BY 4.0 licence of the rest of this repository. CC BY-SA images: derivatives must be shared under the same licence.

Images were checked for burned-in names, dates or record numbers; one candidate effusion film was rejected because it showed a name and study date.
